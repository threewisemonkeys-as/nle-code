NetHack, played blind

A coding agent playing NetHack through the terminal, as it ships: an action is one keystroke, menus and --More-- are its own problem, and the brief never says what the game is. Pick a run, then scrub it — beside each screen is the plan that batch was given, the key it played, and the work the session did in its workspace before playing it.
